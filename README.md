# kas'Jam

A private chord app for jamming with friends. This page only holds the app file.

**Install:** download `kasJam-0.38.apk` on the phone and open it. Android asks once to allow installing from your browser or files app.

**Updates:** kas'Jam checks here by itself when it opens and offers "Update" when a newer version is ready.

Newest version: **0.38**
- YouTube search as from Mauritius; songs like yours come first.

From 0.37:
- Cleaner Settings. Tap the version number to look for an update.

From 0.36:
- The place to search (All, My songs, YouTube, SoundCloud) is chosen inside the search bar.
- Tapping a song while another plays adds it to Up next.

From 0.35:
- New home page with four big buttons, and Carry on with your last song.
- One Music screen for your songs, YouTube and SoundCloud, with one search.
- Up next without a limit, and a History tab in the queue panel.
- Smarter chord finding (better with singing), gapless loops.

kas'Jam includes open-source parts: NewPipe Extractor (GPL-3.0), youtubedl-android (GPL-3.0) with yt-dlp (Unlicense), ZXing (Apache-2.0). Their source code, and kas'Jam's own, is available on request from the owner of this page.
